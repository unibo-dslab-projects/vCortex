Wall or furniture? Six rooms, seen from above

How split_walls_objects sorts straight lines into walls and objects. Each room is 5 × 4 m and drawn to scale; the numbers are examples, not measurements.

Rule 1: both sides

If the robot stood on both sides of a line (within 5 m), the line is inside the room, so it cannot be a wall.

Rule 2: the wardrobe rule

If a parallel line shows up behind a one-sided line, up to 1.5 m back and overlapping it (±1.0 m), the back one is the wall and the front one is furniture.

Objects

Points more than 0.10 m from every wall face are grouped into clusters (≥ 20 points, 0.10 m apart). Each cluster becomes an object.
